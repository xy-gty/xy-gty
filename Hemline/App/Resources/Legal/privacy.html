Privacy notice for Hemline

Last updated 16 October 2026. This page ships inside the app. A public web address for it has not been set.

What never has to leave the phone

Garment photos, closet tags, outfit boards, and the morning briefing you generate stay in Hemline’s local store. Hemline is a stylist for clothes you already own. It is not a shop, and it does not sell your wardrobe to a retailer.

If you turn coaching on

Only after you allow sharing does Hemline send the fields shown on the consent sheet — typically a description of the piece, the occasion, and the text of your question — to DeepSeek. Revoke that permission in Settings and later sends stop. Camera and photo library are for cataloguing garments. Microphone and speech recognition are for asking what to wear out loud.

What we do not do

No advertising identifier, no tracking prompt, no silent upload of the closet.
