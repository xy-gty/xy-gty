Workrest privacy

Interval sessions, form photos, posture notes, work and rest blocks, and coach turns are stored on the device.

Workrest is an interval-training form coach. It is not the knitting app it may have been confused with in an older draft. Photos of your body during a set are sensitive. They stay local unless you allow a coaching send.

An allowed send goes to DeepSeek and is limited to the fields on the consent sheet: a description of the form photo, the block you are in, and the question or transcript you submit. Revoke sharing in Settings.

Camera and Photos capture the rep. Microphone and speech recognition are for interval callouts and spoken questions. No advertising track. Remote privacy URL: not configured.
