Workrest — training terms

Workrest calls work and rest and comments on the shape of a rep. It is not a doctor, a physiotherapist, or a certified coach standing in the room. Stop if something hurts. The app cannot see pain.

Timers and posture notes are training aids. They do not clear you for a sport, a competition, or a medical program.

You confirm a session note before it is kept. These terms are bundled; no external terms URL is set.
