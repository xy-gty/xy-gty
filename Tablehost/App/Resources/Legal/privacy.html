Tablehost privacy

Rulebook pages, table photos, component notes, setup zones, and the host lines you play stay on the device.

Someone else’s living room may be in a table photo. Tablehost does not post that photo. An AI ruling is sent to DeepSeek only if you allow it, and only as the fields on the consent sheet: page or table description, the question, and a speech transcript. Revoke in Settings.

Camera and Photos capture the book and the table. Microphone and speech recognition are for asking “can I play this” while you set up. No advertising track. Remote privacy URL: not set.
