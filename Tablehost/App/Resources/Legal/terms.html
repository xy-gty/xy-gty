Tablehost terms

Tablehost explains setup and talks through a ruling from pages and photos you provide. It is not the publisher of the game, not an official judge, and not a scorebook. House rules at your table win over the app.

A chat answer can misread a page. Check the printed rule when the stake of the argument matters. Do not upload a rulebook you do not have the right to photograph for your own use.

These terms are in-app. No hosted terms URL.
