Racknote privacy

非侍酒认证 / not a sommelier certification. 非投资建议 / not investment advice.

Label photos, bin assignments, drinking-window notes, and pairing chats stay in the cellar file on this device. A cellar list is a private inventory. Racknote does not publish it or price it.

If you allow coaching, DeepSeek receives the label fields named on the consent sheet — not the whole photo library. Revoke in Settings. Camera and Photos read the label. Microphone and speech recognition are for asking which bottle to pull while you are in the racks.

For adults. No advertising identifier. Remote privacy URL: not set.
