Countroom practice terms

Countroom is a practice coach: warm-ups, count-ins, a drone or click, and optional pitch feedback. It is not a tuner you should trust for a recording session’s final pitch, and it is not a hearing test or clinical tool.

A plan becomes part of the session only when you keep it. Discard does not write it. You bring your own sheet music; the app does not supply copyrighted parts.

These terms are stored locally. An external terms link is not configured.
