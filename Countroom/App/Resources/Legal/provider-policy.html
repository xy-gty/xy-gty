DeepSeek Provider Policy (in-app)

Countroom’s outbound practice coaching uses DeepSeek when a key is configured and you grant consent (countroom.consent.v1).

Fields that may be sent: sheet_photo_desc, instrument_label, user_goal, speech_transcript, prior_plan_steps, coach_question, user_feedback.

This page is independent of Countroom’s product Privacy Policy. Revoke consent in Settings to stop sends immediately. Local practice features remain available.
