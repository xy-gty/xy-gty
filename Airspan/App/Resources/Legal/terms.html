How you may use Airspan

Airspan is a duct-sizing coach. It talks through airflow, friction, and velocity and draws a size. It does not stamp a drawing, replace a mechanical engineer, or certify compliance with a building code.

Field conditions — actual static pressure, fittings you did not photograph, and the authority having jurisdiction — stay your responsibility. Confirm a size before you cut metal.

Coaching output is a draft until you keep it. Discard leaves the job file unchanged.

In-app terms only. No external terms address is configured.
