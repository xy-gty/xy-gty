SnagCue AI Privacy

SnagCue AI stores audits, walkthrough photos, snag drafts, and punch items on your device.

Optional AI features may send walkthrough_photo_bytes, punch_item_fields, chat_text, and snag_labels to DeepSeek only after you allow AI data sharing. You can revoke consent in Settings to stop outbound sends immediately.

Guidance only — not a building-code or legal certification. External privacy URL is not configured in this local_only delivery.
