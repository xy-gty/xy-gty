Onionreel privacy

Frames

The sequence you capture, onion-skin guides, shot plans, and countdown lines are kept on device. Stop-motion frames are your artwork.

Coach

DeepSeek is used only after you allow it, and only for the shot-plan fields listed on the consent sheet (frame description, timing note, transcript). Revoke sharing in Settings.

Hands

Camera and Photos build the sequence. Microphone and speech recognition take interval notes and play the countdown so you can stay on the puppet. Onionreel does not record the room between takes.

No ad tracking. This notice is inside the app. A remote privacy URL is not configured.
