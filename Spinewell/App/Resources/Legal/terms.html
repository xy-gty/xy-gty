Spinewell terms

Spinewell is a coach for a personal bookshelf: identify a copy you photographed, remember where it sits, and talk about what to read next. It is not a shop catalogue, not a full-text reader, and not a tool for grading homework or copying a book.

Title guesses can miss a similar jacket. You confirm a book before it is filed. Do not photograph stock you do not own in order to build a pirate library.
