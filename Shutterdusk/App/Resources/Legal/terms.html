Shutterdusk terms

Suggestions about shutter, aperture, ISO, and noise are coaching for a night frame. They are not a warranty that a camera body will perform, not a calibrated meter certificate, and not permission to shoot where you are not allowed to be.

Long exposures need a tripod and your own judgment about traffic, weather, and cold. The app will not be liable for a missed frame or a wrong guess about noise.
