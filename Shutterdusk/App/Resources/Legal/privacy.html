Shutterdusk privacy

Night frames, exposure findings, noise notes, and the settings you dictate (ISO, shutter, focus) remain on device.

This app coaches long exposures after dark. It is not a sun-path planner. A frame can show a private place at night; Shutterdusk does not offer that frame to a network unless you allow one coaching request.

Allowed requests go to DeepSeek with the fields on the consent sheet — frame description, the exposure note, your question or transcript. Revoke in Settings.

Camera and Photos bring the frame in. Microphone and speech recognition are for asking about a setting while you stay at the tripod. No advertising identifier. Remote privacy URL: not set.
