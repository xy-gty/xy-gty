Bloomkettle privacy

Bloomkettle is a home pour-over coach. Beans, grounds, bloom, the pour, crema, recipes, and the next-pour note stay on the device. This app does not time footraces and does not store finish-line photos.

If you allow coaching, DeepSeek receives the brew fields on the consent sheet — a description of the coffee photo, ratio or pour question, and a transcript such as a spoken “next pour.” Revoke sharing in Settings before the kettle boils.

Camera and Photos look at the cup and the dripper. Microphone and speech recognition speak grams and seconds while both hands are on the kettle. No alcohol-service data. No advertising identifier. Remote privacy URL: not set.
