Gillnote terms

非可食认证 / not an edibility certification.

Gillnote compares lookalikes and habitat from photos you take. A name on the screen is not permission to cook, sell, or taste the mushroom. Misidentification can be poisonous. If you cannot afford to be wrong, leave it.

The app is a field notebook with a coach. It is not a foraging license and not a toxicology service. You accept that the coach can be confidently wrong.
