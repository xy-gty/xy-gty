DuctCue Privacy

DuctCue keeps job photos, duct runs, size diagrams, and field steps on your device by default.

If you allow AI coaching, selected chat text, airflow numbers, duct photo bytes, duct run tags, job summary, and sizing context may be sent to DeepSeek for inference. You can revoke sharing anytime in Settings.

External privacy/support URLs are not configured in this local build (replace before submission).
