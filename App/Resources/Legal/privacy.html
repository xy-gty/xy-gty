PitchCue Privacy

PitchCue stores practice sessions, sheet photos, plans, coach turns, audio-bed settings, and pitch samples on your device.

Optional AI coaching may send sheet_photo_desc, instrument_label, user_goal, speech_transcript, prior_plan_steps, coach_question, and user_feedback to DeepSeek only after you allow AI data sharing (disclosure pitchcue.consent.v1). Revoke in Settings to stop outbound sends immediately.

Coaching aid only — not a medical or professional tuner replacement. External privacy URL is not configured in this local_only delivery (gap).
