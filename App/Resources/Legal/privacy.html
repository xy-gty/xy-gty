Spinewell privacy

Cover and spine photos, shelf places, reading notes, and “where is that book” answers stay on the device. Your shelves are a private list. Spinewell does not scrape a bookstore and does not upload a library catalogue.

A coaching send, after you allow it, goes to DeepSeek with the book fields on the consent sheet — title clues from the photo, your question, a transcript. It does not include the text of the book. Revoke in Settings.

Camera and Photos identify the copy you own. Microphone and speech recognition read a blurb or help you find the spine. No advertising track. Remote privacy URL: not set.
