Stirboard privacy

Bottle, label, and garnish photos, recipe notes, and spoken pour steps stay on the device.

The bar in the photo is your home. Stirboard does not publish it. DeepSeek receives a drink question only after you allow sharing, and only the fields on the consent sheet: label description, what you have on hand, and a transcript. Revoke in Settings.

Camera and Photos identify the bottle. Microphone and speech recognition call shake and stir steps while your hands are on the tin. No ad tracking. This notice is local; a remote privacy URL is not configured.
