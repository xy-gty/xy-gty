Fieldmark privacy

Bird photos, field-mark notes, lookalike comparisons, and the spoken name stay on the device.

Where you stood when you took the picture can be sensitive. Fieldmark does not build a public hotspot map. A coaching send, if you allow one, goes to DeepSeek with the still description, the marks you asked about, and a transcript. The consent sheet is the inventory. Settings revokes it.

Camera and Photos are the bird. Microphone and speech recognition let you ask about a lookalike without looking down for long. No advertising identifier. Remote privacy URL: not set.
