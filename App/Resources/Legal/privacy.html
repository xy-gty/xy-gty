Sunbrief and your locations

Scout frames, scene cards, sun and moon windows, overlay pins, and shoot notes stay on the phone unless you explicitly share a coaching request.

A briefing can describe where you meant to stand. Treat that as sensitive. Sunbrief does not publish a map of your stops and does not run ad tracking.

When AI is allowed, DeepSeek may receive scene labels, shot notes, the timing window you asked about, and the transcript of a dictated note. The consent sheet is the list. Revoke it in Settings.

Camera and Photos are for the place and the frame. Microphone and speech recognition are for shoot notes and the next-stop briefing. This page is the privacy notice; a remote URL is unset.
