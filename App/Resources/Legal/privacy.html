FormCue Privacy

FormCue stores projects, WIP photos, stitch cues, annotations, coach turns, and pattern notes on your device.

Optional AI coaching may send wip_photo_descriptor, recognized_stitch_summary, current_row, next_row_intent, pattern_note_text, chat_text, and voice_transcript to DeepSeek only after you allow AI data sharing (disclosure formcue.consent.v1). Revoke in Settings to stop outbound sends immediately.

Weave coach aid only. External privacy URL is not configured in this local_only delivery (gap).
