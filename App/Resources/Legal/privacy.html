ScoutCue Privacy

ScoutCue stores scout shots, scene cards, plans, timing windows, overlay marks, coach turns, shoot notes, and briefing clips on your device.

Optional AI coaching may send scene_labels, shot_meta, plan_windows, user_chat_text, voice_transcript, and overlay_notes to DeepSeek only after you allow AI data sharing (disclosure scoutcue.consent.v1). Revoke in Settings to stop outbound sends immediately.

Scouting aid only — not an observatory or professional light meter. External privacy URL is not configured in this local_only delivery (gap).
