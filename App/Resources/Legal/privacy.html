Gillnote privacy

非可食认证 / not an edibility certification. Gillnote never certifies a mushroom as safe to eat.

Cap, gill, and pore photos, habitat notes, lookalike comparisons, and field briefs stay on the device. A foraging photo can reveal a patch. Gillnote does not share that patch unless you allow a single coaching request.

That request goes to DeepSeek and is limited to the morphology fields on the consent sheet. Revoke sharing in Settings. Camera and Photos are the specimen. Microphone and speech recognition are the field brief, so you can listen with dirty hands.

No ad tracking. This page is the privacy notice. A remote URL is not set.
