Airspan job-site privacy

  1. On the device. Duct photos, run sketches, CFM notes, friction and velocity figures, and the size diagram stay local unless you opt in.
  2. Optional coach. If you allow it, Airspan sends the declared job fields (photo description, airflow numbers, your spoken question) to DeepSeek. The consent sheet lists them before the first send. Settings can revoke that immediately.
  3. Sensors. The camera and photo library capture registers and as-built drawings. The microphone and speech recognition exist so you can ask a sizing question from a ladder without typing.
  4. Not a filing cabinet for a client you did not name. Job photos can show other people’s property. You are the one who chooses to import them.

No ad tracking. This notice is bundled with the app; a remote privacy URL is not set.
