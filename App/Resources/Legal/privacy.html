KnotCue Privacy

KnotCue keeps practice photos, knot stages, step overlays, and hands-free plans on your device by default.

If you allow AI coaching, selected chat text, knot photo bytes, stage tags, haul context, practice summary, and speech transcript may be sent to DeepSeek for inference. You can revoke sharing anytime in Settings.

External privacy/support URLs are not configured in this local build (replace before submission).
