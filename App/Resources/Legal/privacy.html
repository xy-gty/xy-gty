Countroom — what the practice room keeps

Sheets, exercise photos, warm-up plans, count-in settings, drone and metronome choices, and any pitch sample you record remain on this device.

The coach is optional

DeepSeek receives a practice request only after you allow it. Typical fields: a description of the page, the instrument, your goal, and a transcript of what you said. The consent sheet names the set. Turning sharing off in Settings ends further sends.

Microphone

The mic is for pitch and rhythm feedback in the room. Speech recognition turns a spoken goal into text you can still edit. Neither runs as a background recorder for its own sake.
