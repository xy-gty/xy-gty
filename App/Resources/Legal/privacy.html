Mattebox privacy

Stills, safe-frame and thirds overlays, shot-size notes, and on-set transcripts stay on device.

A frame from a working set can show people and locations. Mattebox keeps it local. Coaching sends to DeepSeek happen only after consent and only with the fields the sheet discloses: still description, shot-size plan, and your note or transcript. Revoke in Settings between setups.

Camera and Photos are the still. Microphone and speech recognition are the on-set note, because typing between takes is slow. No ad tracking. This privacy page is bundled; a remote URL is not set.
