FolioCue terms

个人藏书教练 / personal bookshelf coach

非书店目录爬取 / not bookstore catalog scraping

非全文盗版阅读器 / not a full-text piracy reader

非课业批改 / not homework grading

Local legal page for development. Remote URL is UNKNOWN (gap). FolioCue is a free local AI personal bookshelf coach for cover/spine ID, TBR/reading chat, annotated shelf cards, and TTS find-my-book callouts.

Provider for outbound AI (when configured): DeepSeek. Fields and disclosure version appear in the in-app consent sheet before any send. Revoke anytime in Settings.

Camera, Photos, Microphone, and Speech Recognition extend the shelf coach as described in Info.plist usage strings. Stills stay user-owned on device unless you opt in to share listed fields with DeepSeek.

No App Tracking Transparency, no StoreKit paywall in this UltraLite build.
