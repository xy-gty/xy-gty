Loopwell terms

Loopwell coaches knitting and crochet from your own photos and notes. A stitch call can be wrong. Count the loops before you rip a row back on the app’s word alone.

Nothing here is textile grading, a pattern license, or medical advice about hands or posture. You own the project photos. Do not import a pattern you do not have the right to use.

Keep a stitch line if you want it saved. Discard leaves the project as it was.
