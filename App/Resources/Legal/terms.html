Sunbrief — planner, not a guide service

Timing windows are estimates for a photograph, not a navigation instruction and not a guarantee the sun will clear a ridge you did not show the app. You choose whether a location is safe to reach and legal to photograph.

Golden-hour marks and spoken briefings are planning aids. Check the sky yourself. Do not treat Sunbrief as an emergency or travel service.
