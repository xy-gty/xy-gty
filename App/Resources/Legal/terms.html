Onionreel terms

Onionreel plans stop-motion timing and shows the previous frame under the next one. A countdown and a shot plan are production notes, not a film-school grade and not a claim that the finished movie will cut together.

You are responsible for the puppets, sets, and any people who appear in a frame, and for the rights to what you animate.

Keep a plan if you want it stored. Local terms; no external URL.
