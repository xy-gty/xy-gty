Broodcheck terms

Broodcheck helps you read a brood frame: brood, stores, pest signs, and a suggested next action. It is an inspection coach, not a veterinarian and not a guarantee the colony is healthy or diseased.

Treatment decisions, local beekeeping rules, and whether to destroy comb are yours. A spoken note is a draft until you keep it in the log.
