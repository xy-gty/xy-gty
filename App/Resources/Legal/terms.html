Racknote terms

非侍酒认证 / not a sommelier certification. 非投资建议 / not investment advice. Adults 21 and over.

Racknote files a bottle from its label and talks about a drinking window and a plate. A window is a guess, not a cellar valuation and not a promise the wine is sound, authentic, or worth what you paid.

Do not use it to decide an auction bid. You are responsible for alcohol service in your house. The app does not verify age at the door beyond this notice.
