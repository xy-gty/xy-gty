Hemline terms of use

Hemline suggests combinations from the clothes you photograph. A suggestion is a styling idea, not a promise that a brand, size, or shop listing is accurate, and not an endorsement of any label.

You decide what goes into the closet and what you actually wear. Keep, discard, or edit a look before it is saved. Weather and occasion notes are planning aids.

Do not upload photos of people you do not have permission to catalogue. The app is for personal wardrobe coaching.

These terms are the in-app copy. A hosted terms URL is not configured in this build.
